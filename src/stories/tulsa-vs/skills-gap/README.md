# Tulsa vs The Skills Gap

## Series Context
**"Tulsa vs"** — Tulsa takes on abstract challenges, systemic problems, and industry-wide issues. Not city vs city — it's Tulsa vs the problem itself.

## Hook
"Everyone talks about the skills gap. Tulsa is actually closing it."

## The Problem (National Context)
- Manufacturing has 600,000+ unfilled jobs nationwide
- 2.1 million manufacturing jobs could go unfilled by 2030 (Deloitte/NAM)
- 45% of manufacturers turned down business due to worker shortages
- Average age of skilled tradespeople: 56+ years old
- Trade school enrollment declined for decades while student debt exploded

## Tulsa's Answer
- **Tulsa Tech** — One of the largest career tech systems in the country
- **Tulsa Welding School** — Premier welding/HVAC training
- **OSU-IT / TCC** — Manufacturing and industrial tech programs
- **Tulsa Innovation Labs** — Employer-driven workforce development
- **Industry partnerships** — AAON, QuikTrip, aerospace cluster all investing in pipelines
- **Cost advantage** — Training here costs a fraction of coastal programs

## Visualization Concepts

### 1. "The Gap" (Scroll-driven)
- Start with national 600K unfilled jobs stat
- Zoom into Oklahoma/Tulsa
- Show local training pipeline filling the gap
- End with "X graduates per year → Y% placement rate"

### 2. "Two Paths" Side-by-side
- Path A: 4-year degree → $50K debt → job search
- Path B: Tulsa Tech → 18 months → $60K job → $0 debt
- Animated timeline showing when each person hits net positive

### 3. "The Pipeline" Sankey
- Flow from: high school → training programs → employers
- Show actual numbers: enrollment → completion → placement → retention
