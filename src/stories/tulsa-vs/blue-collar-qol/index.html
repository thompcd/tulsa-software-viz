The Blue Collar Reality

Blue collar work built America. Manufacturing, construction, transportation — the backbone of the economy.

But in 2026, can these jobs still support a family?

What Blue Collar Pays in Tulsa

These are real wages for real jobs in the Tulsa metro area.

The average across blue collar occupations: $44,088

Source: BLS Occupational Employment Statistics, 2024

But What Does a Family Need?

MIT's Living Wage Calculator says a family of four (2 adults, 1 working, 2 kids) needs:

$79,924/year

Just to cover the basics. No vacations. No savings. Just survival.

Source: MIT Living Wage Calculator, 2025

The Gap

Average blue collar salary: $44,088

Living wage needed: $79,924

Gap: $35,836 (44.8% short)

Where the Paycheck Goes

On a $44,088 salary, here's what a family needs to spend:

  • 🚗 Transportation: 39.6%
  • 🏠 Housing: 31.9%
  • 🍔 Food: 28.1%
  • 🏥 Medical: 22.3%

The Math Doesn't Work

Those percentages add up to 122% of income.

Single-income blue collar families are underwater before they start.

That's the squeeze.

But Here's Tulsa's Edge

Housing in Tulsa costs 22.7% less than the national average.

That's $4,114/year back in your pocket.

It's not everything. But it's something.

Housing: Tulsa vs National

Tulsa $14,047
National Avg $18,161

For a working family, that savings is real money.

Source: MIT Living Wage Calculator, 2025

The Two-Income Solution

When both adults work, the math changes:

  • Combined income: $88,176
  • Living wage needed: $105,352
  • Gap shrinks to: $17,176

Still short, but manageable.

The Bottom Line

Blue collar families face real pressure everywhere.

Tulsa isn't perfect. But with 22% cheaper housing and a lower cost of living, it's one of the better hands you can be dealt.

The American Dream isn't dead here. It just takes two paychecks.
