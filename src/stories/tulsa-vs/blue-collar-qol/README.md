# Tulsa vs The Blue Collar Squeeze

## Hook
"A welder in Tulsa earns $45,800. A family of four needs $79,924. Where does that leave working families?"

## Target Audience
- Blue collar workers and families
- People considering relocation
- Economic development audiences
- Policy makers focused on working families

## The Story

### The Problem
Blue collar wages haven't kept pace with the cost of living. A single-income blue collar family faces a **$35,836 gap** between what they earn and what they need.

### Tulsa's Answer
- **Housing costs 22.7% less** than national average ($4,114/year savings)
- **Lower overall cost of living** keeps more money in pockets
- **Dual-income households** can actually make it work

### The Data

**Living Wage Requirement (2 adults, 1 working, 2 kids):**
| Location | Hourly | Annual |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Tulsa | $38.42 | $79,924 |
| Texas Avg | $39.29 | $81,719 |
| San Antonio | $38.56 | $80,208 |

**Typical Blue Collar Salaries in Tulsa:**
| Occupation | Annual |
|------------|--------|
| Truck Driver | $48,500 |

| Machinist | $46,300 |
| Construction Laborer | $38,500 |
| Production Worker | $36,200 |
| Warehouse Worker | $35,800 |
| **Average** | **$44,088** |

**Paycheck Breakdown ($44,088/year):**
| Expense | Monthly | % of Gross |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Housing | $1,171 | 31.9% |
| Transportation | $1,454 | 39.6% |
| Food | $1,034 | 28.1% |
| Medical | $821 | 22.3% |

*Note: Percentages exceed 100% — this is the problem.*

## Visualization Concepts

### 1. "The Paycheck Pie" (Animated)
Show a paycheck splitting into expense categories. Animate the slices growing until they exceed the pie — visualizing the squeeze.

### 2. "The Gap" (Bar Chart)
Side-by-side: What you earn vs what you need
- Blue collar average: $44,088
- Living wage needed: $79,924
- Gap: $35,836 (44.8%)

### 3. "Where It Goes" (Stacked Bar / Sankey)
Dollar bill breaks into:
- Housing: 32¢
- Transportation: 40¢  
- Food: 28¢
- Medical: 22¢
- Total: $1.22 needed for every $1.00 earned

### 4. "Tulsa vs National" (Comparison Bars)
| Category | Tulsa | National | Savings |
|----------|-------|----------|---------|
| Housing | $14,047 | $18,161 | $4,114 |
| Food | $12,405 | $11,319 | -$1,086 |
| Transportation | $17,444 | $17,050 | -$394 |
| **Net Savings** | | | **$2,634** |

### 5. "The Two-Income Solution" (Slider/Interactive)
Show how the math changes:
- 1 income: Gap of $35,836
- 2 incomes: Gap shrinks to $17,176
- Living becomes possible

## Scrollytelling Structure

1. **Hook**: Welder story, the gap
2. **The Numbers**: Animate expense breakdown
3. **The Squeeze**: Percentages exceeding 100%
4. **The National Picture**: Compare to other cities
5. **Tulsa's Edge**: Housing savings
6. **The Path Forward**: Dual income math
7. **Conclusion**: "Tulsa isn't perfect, but the math works"
