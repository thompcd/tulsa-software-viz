# An Italian Company Bet $360 Million on Tulsa

## Hook
"When Sofidel — an Italian manufacturer — needed to build a 1.8 million sq ft plant, they didn't pick New York, Chicago, or LA. They picked Tulsa."

## Target Audience
- Manufacturing decision makers
- Site selection professionals
- Economic developers
- International business audiences

## Visualization Concept
Scale comparison showing the magnitude of the investment:
- 1.8M sq ft = X football fields
- Before/after aerial imagery
- Map showing why Tulsa (logistics, labor, costs)
- Timeline of investment to operation

## Key Data Points
- $360 million investment (2020)
- 1.8 million square feet
- One of largest single manufacturing investments in recent Tulsa history
- Italian tissue paper manufacturer (global company choosing Oklahoma)

## Data Sources
- Tulsa Regional Chamber press releases
- Sofidel company announcements
- Local news coverage

## CTA Angle
Why international companies are choosing Tulsa: cost of operations, workforce quality, logistics advantages. What this means for the local tech ecosystem that supports them.
