# Every $1 Creates $2.60

## Hook
"$23.6 billion. That's what Oklahoma manufacturing adds to the economy. But the multiplier effect makes it $61 billion."

## Target Audience
- Economic development professionals
- Manufacturing advocates
- Tulsa/Oklahoma business community
- Policy makers

## Visualization Concept
Cascading multiplier animation. Start with a dollar bill, show it rippling into supply chain, services, local economy. Make it Tulsa-specific: "$23.6B creates $61.4B in total economic activity."

Options:
- Sankey diagram showing flow of economic impact
- Animated cascade/ripple effect
- Tree/branch visualization

## Key Data Points
- Every $1 in manufacturing generates $2.60 in additional economic activity (NAM)
- Oklahoma manufacturing: $23.6 billion value added = 8.7% of state GDP
- 141,500 people employed by 3,000+ manufacturers
- Average manufacturing salary: $87,063 (48% premium over non-farm average)

## Data Sources
- National Association of Manufacturers (2024 Oklahoma facts)
- Bureau of Labor Statistics
- Tulsa Regional Chamber

## CTA Angle
Investing in manufacturing efficiency (lean + software) has outsized economic returns for the region.
