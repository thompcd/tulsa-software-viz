# Manufacturing's $87K Secret

## Hook
"The average Oklahoma manufacturing worker earns $87,063. That's 48% more than the average worker. And Tulsa is the epicenter."

## Target Audience
- Career changers
- Workforce development
- Parents/educators
- Policy makers

## Visualization Concept
Bar chart comparison: manufacturing vs non-manufacturing wages by state/region. Challenge the "blue collar = low pay" stereotype.

Options:
- Side-by-side bar chart with 48% premium highlighted
- Wage growth over time (manufacturing vs average)
- Skills/education vs salary scatter

## Key Data Points
- OK manufacturing salary: $87,063
- OK non-farm average: $58,712
- 48% premium for manufacturing workers
- 141,500 manufacturing jobs in Oklahoma
- $23.6 billion value added to OK economy

## Data Sources
- National Association of Manufacturers (2024)
- Bureau of Labor Statistics

## CTA Angle
Manufacturing isn't the blue-collar stereotype. It's high-tech, high-paying, and centered in places like Tulsa.
