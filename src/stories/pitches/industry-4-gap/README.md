# Only 20% Have Scaled Industry 4.0

## Hook
"70% of manufacturers use some lean principles. But only 20% have successfully digitized them. That gap is a $500 billion opportunity."

## Target Audience
- Manufacturing IT leaders
- Operations executives
- Digital transformation teams
- Industrial software vendors

## Visualization Concept
Funnel/pipeline chart showing dropoff: 70% use lean → 25% fully implemented → 20% digitized. Show where companies get stuck.

Options:
- Funnel visualization with dropoff percentages
- Maturity model progression chart
- Gap analysis: traditional lean vs digital lean

## Key Data Points
- 70% of manufacturing companies report using some lean principles
- Only 25-30% have fully implemented lean practices
- Only 15-20% have successfully scaled Industry 4.0 pilots
- IIoT in manufacturing market expected to exceed $500 billion by 2030
- Real-time monitoring reduces downtime by 30-50%
- Predictive maintenance reduces costs by 25-30%

## Data Sources
- AME/Shingo surveys
- Grand View Research (IIoT market)
- McKinsey manufacturing reports
- Industry 4.0 adoption studies

## CTA Angle
The bridge between traditional lean and digital lean is where the biggest opportunity sits. Most companies are stuck at proof-of-concept.
