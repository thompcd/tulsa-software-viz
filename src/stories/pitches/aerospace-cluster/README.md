# Tulsa's $11 Billion Sky

## Hook
"Guess where the world's largest commercial aircraft maintenance facility is. Tulsa."

## Target Audience
- Aerospace industry professionals
- Software developers in aviation
- Economic development audiences
- Manufacturing/operations leaders

## Visualization Concept
Map-based infographic showing Tulsa's aerospace cluster with company locations, employee counts, and economic impact. Could include:
- Aerial view of AA maintenance base with overlaid stats
- Company cluster map with size proportional to employees
- Timeline of aerospace growth in Tulsa

## Key Data Points
- $11 billion annual economic impact
- American Airlines Tulsa Maintenance Base — largest in the world
- 800+ planes/year, 5,500+ technicians
- Major players: NORDAM, Lufthansa Technik, L3 Harris, Spirit AeroSystems

## Data Sources
- Tulsa Regional Chamber
- Tulsa's Future economic data
- American Airlines public reports
- Company websites

## CTA Angle
Every one of those operations runs on software. Maintenance tracking, predictive analytics, supply chain — most held together with legacy systems. The companies solving that won't come from Silicon Valley.
